# Colour diagrams

Five hand-drawn figures for the parts of colour talk that a paragraph argues about and a picture settles: the artists' wheel and its tiers, the harmony schemes drawn on it, what happens when that wheel is laid beside the sRGB one, what a wheel leaves out that a solid keeps, and the three mixing rules that get confused with one another. The captions say where each one is a convention rather than a measurement.
